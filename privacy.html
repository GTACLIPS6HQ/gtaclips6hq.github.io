Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

GTACLIPS6HQ (“the app”) is a personal tool used only by the owner of the GTACLIPS6HQ accounts to publish short gaming videos to those accounts. It has no public sign-up and no other users.

What the app accesses

  • Account access tokens for the GTACLIPS6HQ accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Twitch and Telegram, granted by the owner through each platform's official sign-in. They are used only to upload videos and read the basic account information needed to do so.
  • Public Twitch chat messages and public stream video of streams being clipped, used only to detect highlights and create clips.

Where data is stored

All data, including access tokens, stays on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Video and text are sent only to the platforms the owner publishes to, and short text prompts may be sent to Google Gemini to write video titles.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in each platform's security settings, which makes the stored tokens useless. Deleting the app's data folder removes everything it stored.
